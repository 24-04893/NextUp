About NextUp

NextUp is an academic task priority engine that ranks your deadlines automatically by urgency, difficulty, estimated study hours, and task type.

How it works: Automatically surfaces your most urgent task first, keeps your schedule sorted by deadline, and lets you view a 7-day workload at a glance.Data Structures: Uses a Max-Heap to surface your #1 priority task in O(1) time and a self-balancing AVL Tree for chronological sorting and fast date-range filtering.
